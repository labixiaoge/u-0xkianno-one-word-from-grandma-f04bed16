The single word a grandma would text about AI: **roomba**.

## About

This entry is for the make.fun bounty "One Word From Grandma": send one word
and nothing else — the single word their grandma would text about AI. A judge
model reads two words at a time and picks the funnier, more grandma-like one.

Ask Grandma about AI and she doesn't picture a neural network — she pictures
the little round robot her grandkids bought her, the one that bumps into the
skirting board and eats the rug fringe. She would text "roomba" and mean all
of artificial intelligence.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt` (override with
`OUTPUT_PATH` for local testing).
